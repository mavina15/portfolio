Summer Assessment Program

Scheduled and ran testing for 147 students across four home languages, then wrote the placement report used to set next year's cohorts.

Goal

Leadership needed every incoming 5th grader and new 6th to 8th grader to finish an English and a Math diagnostic before August scheduling. The results would set each student's placement, build balanced classes, and flag who needed extra support from the first day.

How the program ran

June 29 – July 17, 2026
Jun 29 – Jul 2Prep week

Classroom setup, IT training, roster checks and family notices.

Jul 6 – 10Week 1 testing

Last names A–L. Three classrooms, 20 to 25 students per session.

Jul 13 – 17Week 2 testing

Last names M–Z, plus Week 1 absences. Final report on July 17.

Each day ran from 8:00 breakfast through three testing blocks and lunch to 3:00 pickup. Students could leave the program once both diagnostics were done, and they picked up their school Chromebooks during their week.

Step 2 · Running the diagnostics

Each testing week had its own tracker. Students were pulled from the roster, split into three equal groups for proctors, and logged as each English and Math diagnostic was finished. Rushed or interrupted tests were reset and retaken so every score was valid.

Week 1 · completed both diagnostics59 of 70 · 84%
Week 2 · completed both diagnostics69 of 74 · 93%

Week 1 absences were invited back for Week 2. Students who missed both weeks test in August.

Step 3 · Building balanced cohorts

The results fed a cohort tracker for the 113 incoming 5th graders. The goal was four classes with no cohort stronger or weaker than another, and a similar mix of girls and boys, students with IEPs and 504 plans, Black, Hispanic, White and Asian students, and English learners and English speakers. The sheet compared every cohort side by side and updated as students were moved.

How the cohort tracker works
  • Students: scores, placements and balancing details, with a dropdown to set each student's cohort.
  • Balance: COUNTIFS and AVERAGEIF compare the four cohorts, and each spread turns green when cohorts are within 1 student or 10 points.
  • Cohort lists: each class list fills in automatically for its teacher.
